§ 02 / HIGHEST IMPACT

Tier 1The Big Four.

These produced the largest jumps in HR rate when used as filters. Tier 1 expanded this update — Launch Angle earned its way in.

Pull%≥ 45–50%

Still the strongest linear signal alongside LA. Pulled balls are the ones that leave the yard — squared-up contact in the direction of the shorter foul-pole distance.

At Pull% ≥ 50%, HR rate is 10.92% on n=403. The drop above ≥55% (back to 8.96%) suggests pulled-but-grounder territory — 50% is the sweet spot.

1.68×Lift at ≥50%
Barrel%≥ 12% (elite at ≥20%)

Barrels are the optimal exit-velocity / launch-angle combinations — by definition, the contact most likely to leave the park.

At ≥ 12%: 10.46% rate (1.61×). At ≥ 20%: 13.64% (2.10×) — newly emerged as an elite-tier filter on n=132.

2.10×Lift at ≥20%
Launch Angle ↑ PROMOTED≥ 15° (sweet spot 15–20°)

Promoted from Tier 2 this update. After adding 5/15, LA jumped to the #1 standardized regression coefficient — meaning even when you control for Barrel%, Pull%, and contact quality, LA still has independent explanatory power.

At LA ≥ 15°: 9.56% rate (1.47×). At ≥ 20°: 9.74% (1.50×). Anything below 10° is a red flag — ground balls don't leave the park.

1.50×Lift at ≥20°
Weather Effect≥ 0% (and ideally ≥ +10%)

Wind direction, temperature, humidity. Lift moderated this update but remains real.

At Weather ≥ +10%, HR rate is 17.11% on n=76 (down from 22.4% with a smaller sample, but still 2.63× lift). The non-linearity remains: ≥ 0% gets 1.33×, but ≥ +10% jumps to 2.63×.

§ 05 / LOW IMPACT

Tier 4The Trap.

Don't be fooled. This one keeps looking like it should matter and keeps not mattering.

FB% (Fly Ball Rate)— skip as a filter —

Verdict unchanged after 4 days. The logistic regression assigns FB% a negative coefficient once Pull%, LA, and Barrel% are controlled for. At ≥ 50%, the HR rate is 9.76% (1.50×) — but every gain here is already captured by your other filters.

§ 09 / HONEST CAVEATS

Where This Might Still Be Wrong.

More data has tightened the picture, but the sample is still moderate. Three things to keep in mind.

01 — SAMPLE STILL MODERATE

103 HRs across 4 days is better, but not big. The Premium Screen rests on n=52. The Weather ≥ +10% reading rests on n=76. Worth re-running once we have two weeks of data.

02 — COLLINEARITY PERSISTS

The metrics correlate with each other. Barrel% ↔ HR/FB, Pull% ↔ LA, LA ↔ FB%. Stacking 5+ filters won't multiply lift — diminishing returns kick in around 3–4 filters.

03 — NEW METRICS ARE PROVISIONAL

HardHit% and Combined Effect have only one day of data. Don't use them as primary filters yet. Re-evaluate after a week of accumulation.
